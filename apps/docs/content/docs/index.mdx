> **Original design and engineering by [Gustavo Fior (@gustavo-fior)](https://github.com/gustavo-fior), co-founder [Abdul Haidar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abdulhdr/), and [Foglamp Labs](https://foglamp.dev).** This design system is extracted AS-IS from [Foglamp](https://github.com/foglamp-labs/foglamp). Find Gustavo at [gustavofior.com](https://gustavofior.com), [@heyimgustavo](https://x.com/heyimgustavo), and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gustavofior).

Foglamp gives teams a view into what their AI applications are doing. Its interface brings traces, prompts, evaluations, and workflows together. This project extracts the interface into `@foglamp/design-system` so that its components and visual language can be used outside the original application.

The original design is the contract. The package retains Foglamp's light and dark treatments, layered shadows, progressive squircle corners, form controls, and motion. The portal documents that work and provides a place to explore it. Final visual verification remains part of the project's release work.

Start with [installation](/docs/foundations/installation), then read about the [package architecture](/docs/foundations/architecture) and [design tokens](/docs/foundations/tokens). The [credits](/docs/credits) tell the story of the people behind Foglamp.
