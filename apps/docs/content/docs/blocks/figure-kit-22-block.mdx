Figure Kit · 22 Source Figures selects a figure by section and variant ID. The presets show a cost chart, trace X-ray, and quality review.

## Package entry

`@foglamp/design-system/blocks/marketing/figures/figure-kit-22-block` exports this block. The interactive preview above comes from `apps/docs/src/stories/blocks/figure-kit-22-block.story.tsx`.

## API reference

| Entry | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Story arguments | `section: 'costs' | 'traces' | 'quality'`, `variantId: string`; presets are `costs` with `chart`, `traces` with `xray`, and `quality` with `review`. |
| Fixture and callback wiring | The story exposes `section` (`costs`, `traces`, or `quality`) and `variantId`. The component selects one of the 22 source figures for that pair. The workbench offers three presets: `costs` with `chart`, `traces` with `xray`, and `quality` with `review`. |
| Registry item | `block-marketing-figures-figure-kit-22`. The portal payload is `/r/block-marketing-figures-figure-kit-22.json`. |
